# ÉPURE COLLECTION — French Maison Brand System v2.0.0

## Identité
- ÉPURE COLLECTION: 가상의 프랑스 감성 여성복 메종. 파리의 살롱과 프랑스식 미학을 참조하되 실제 프랑스 생산 또는 법인 설립을 주장하지 않음.
- Signature: L’élégance en toute simplicité.
- French microcopy: La Maison, Le Journal, Savoir-Faire, Salon Privé, Sur Mesure, Concierge.
- Visual DNA: Haussmann windows, quiet Paris apartment, natural stone, restrained gold, ivory/camel and oxblood accent.
- Typeface: Cormorant Garamond italic/editorial + Jost functional.
- Color: Paper #fcfaf6, écru #f5f0e9, espresso #302120, wine #643a3f, pale taupe #e4d7cc.
- Logo: assets/brand/epure-selected-collection-original.jpg (user-selected), assets/epure-wordmark.png (derived PNG), assets/epure-logo.jpg (full selection). Preserve originals.
- Moodboard: assets/brand/epure-selected-moodboard-original.png (user-selected), assets/epure-brand-moodboard.webp (web-optimized).
- Product prices, provenance, salons and appointments are illustrative; no paid checkout or real bookings.

## Page routing
- `/` hero/editorial/movement/collection/materials/private salon/journal.
- `/maison.html` history, selected logo and moodboard (fictional concept clear).
- `/collection.html` seasonal collection.
- `/material.html` textiles and craftsmanship.
- `/fitting.html` fitting guide.
- `/care.html` concierge.
- `/salon.html` VIP consultation demo.
- `/journal.html` editorial content and article pages.

## Production controls
Verify legal brand/trademark, manufacturer and material certifications, verified addresses before public sales. All origins must be evidenced. Do not present the demo salon form or local-only newsletter as actually accepting customer reservations.
